## Overview

Panzer is a free sub-bass sampler built for heavy low-end sounds. It handles root notes and note holds automatically, which makes sustained sub lines quick to write, and has distortion and amplification controls. It runs as a plugin on Windows and macOS and is free for personal and commercial use.

## Presets

The preset bank was made with other producers, who are credited by their handles: attackishere, sh1nxo, starboyrob, prod_comet, themed1c, musicbytovy, 333v0id, aqualil.i and gloomstone_.
