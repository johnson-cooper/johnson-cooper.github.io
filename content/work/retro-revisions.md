## Backported features

- Block of Netherite, crafted from nine diamond blocks.
- Beacon, a full backport with a 50-block radius, smelted from the mod's Lux Aeterna.
- Item frames.
- Daylight and night detectors.

## Original additions

- **Chimney:** a block that emits smoke when powered by redstone.
- **Lux Aeterna:** crafted from nine blocks of Netherite.
- **Iron Boat:** an unbreakable-collision boat that moves fast on ice.
- **Redstone Stairs:** stairs that change direction with a redstone signal.

## Installation

Add the contents of the release zip to `minecraft.jar`, either through a launcher such as Prism or MultiMC, or by hand.
