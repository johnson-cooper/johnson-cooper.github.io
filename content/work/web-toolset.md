## The tools

- **FBXu, an online model viewer:** preview and inspect 3D models in the browser.
- **3D text generator:** make customised 3D text with font and style options.
- **Bootstrap 5 colour theme generator:** pick colours and preview the theme before using it.

They are static pages and still work; they predate this site's redesign and keep their original look.
